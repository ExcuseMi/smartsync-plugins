## 0.1.0 (not tagged yet)
- Events and states for Tasker, MacroDroid and Automate: button press, connect, disconnect, step goal, heart rate and
  battery levels, workout stored, health read; variables per event.
- Actions: notification, watch setting, alarm or timer, read values now; result in `%gs_result` and `%gs_error`.
- The Locale plugin intent contract written out (the Tasker plugin library is GPL-3.0).
